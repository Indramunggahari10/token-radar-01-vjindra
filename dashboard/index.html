How the radar works

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × age × chains

  • Category multiple. The median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the protocols in the same category whose token already trades, winsorized and shrunk toward the global median. Sparse categories take the global median outright.
  • Growth. The 7-day TVL change, clamped and passed through a tanh curve, so one wild week cannot own the board.
  • Age. A seasoning premium for running longer with no token, easing back after year two. Unknown listing dates stay neutral.
  • Chains. A small log premium for multi-chain reach, capped.
  • The screen. Wrapped, staked, bridged and exchange products are set aside, not ranked: they are not protocols waiting on a launch of their own. The 144 set aside are listed below, each with its reason.

Limits, stated plainly: the snapshot carries no yield, no per-chain TVL and no launch history, so the model uses only what the snapshot holds. “Records no token” means the record carries no token — a protocol may still have one its record does not carry. An implied value is a conditional claim, never a prediction that a token launches, or when.
